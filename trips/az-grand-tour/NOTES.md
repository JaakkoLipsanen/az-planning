# AZ Grand Tour - route notes

Fool's Loop -> Queen's Ransom -> Kearny -> Arizona Trail -> Oracle -> Mt Lemmon Control Road -> Summerhaven -> Catalina Highway + Bug Spring -> AZT south (Saguaro NP via the Madrean Rugged Ramble, Patagonia detour) -> Madrean Rugged Ramble via Lochiel -> Tucson

Totals and the section table below are generated by `pnpm trip build az-grand-tour`.

## Files
- `dist/AZ_FoolsLoop_QueensRansom_AZT_Lemmon_MRR_full.gpx` - one track, one segment (best for GPS units), with the resupply / water / camp waypoints from the source routes and these notes.
- `dist/AZ_FoolsLoop_QueensRansom_AZT_Lemmon_MRR_by_section.gpx` - same points, one named track per section.
- The map app exports both, plus one GPX per day of the day plan.
- `maps/` holds the option maps made while planning: the Fool's Loop to Queen's Ransom links, the Kearny and Oracle variants and a route overview.
- `tracks/sources/` holds the original GPX files (bikepacking.com Fools Loop Feb 2021, Queen's Ransom v2026.07, Redington-Lemmon Loop, Madrean Rugged Ramble; AZTR AZT300_SBXL_2026_v3, AZT300_2026_v6, Legacy AZT to Patagonia, Summerhaven detour), plus reference tracks shown on the map only: Sky Islands Odyssey East/West v2023 and the Arizona Trail Association's hiking AZT (passages 1-22, converted from the ATA KMZ). `tracks/connectors/` and `tracks/alternatives/` hold the links routed on OpenStreetMap.

All points taken from a source route are copied verbatim (lat/lon/ele strings unchanged). Only the connectors are new, routed on OpenStreetMap roads/tracks (BRouter or a Dijkstra over an Overpass extract); most carry no elevation, so their climb comes from the Terrarium elevation model.

## Decisions baked into the route
- Fool's Loop ridden in its official (counter-clockwise) direction from the 44th St Sky Train station; left at loop km 420 (Shea Blvd / Camelback Walk, Scottsdale). Link 1b to the Queen's Ransom: Pima Rd south to the Arizona Canal, 3.5 km on the canal bank path (dirt; OSM maps it as an open regional cycle route, but it runs inside the Salt River Pima-Maricopa Indian Community - if it is gated or signed no-trespassing, fall back to link 1a on the map: Pima Rd / Chaparral Rd, +0.8 km, all public roads), then Alma School Rd, McKellips Rd, Center St and McDowell Rd to Desert Trails Park.
- Queen's Ransom joined at Desert Trails Park (QR km 25, where the Hawes singletrack starts) and followed to the ADOT yard water spigot at the Kelvin bridge.
- Kearny out-and-back on E Ray Junction Rd + AZ-177 (10.6 km each way). The dirt shortcut west of Kearny was dropped: it depends on a bridge signed private, and OSM shows a 0.7 km unmapped gap.
- Arizona Trail from the Kelvin bridge southbound using the AZTR AZT300_SBXL_2026_v3 track (identical to the northbound v6 track everywhere ridden except the Summerhaven-Prison Camp section).
- Oracle variant B3: leave the AZT at Hwy 77, Cherry Valley Wash / Powerline trails and E American Ave into town (Patio Market), then Cody Loop Rd, South Wildcat Trail and the Cody Trail back to the AZT at American Flag TH. Circle K / Dollar General are ~2.5 km further west on W American Ave if you need a full grocery.
- Control Road from American Flag TH to Summerhaven taken from the bikepacking.com Redington-Lemmon Loop GPX, then 0.7 km through Summerhaven village (General Store / Cookie Cabin) to the top of the Catalina Highway.
- Summerhaven -> Prison Camp: the paved Catalina Highway (General Hitchcock Hwy) for 21 km to the Bug Spring / Green Mountain trailheads - routed on OpenStreetMap from the General Store to below Soldier Camp, because the AZT300 v6 line there leaves the highway for Marshall Gulch and the Sunset Trail #90 (kept as an alternative) - then the AZT300 v6 points; then the Bug Spring Trail (SB-XL points, 7.6 km, technical with some hike-a-bike) to Prison Camp, followed by the Molino Basin and Bellota trails on the AZT300. The SB-XL variant (upper Control Road, Kellogg, Incinerator Ridge, Green Mountain - 26 km of expert hike-a-bike) is kept as an alternative, as is staying on the highway all the way to Prison Camp.
- From Prison Camp the AZT300 track is followed (Molino, Bellota, Redington Rd, east Tucson, Old Spanish Trail). Where the AZT300 bypasses Saguaro National Park on roads (8.6 km), the route switches to the Madrean Rugged Ramble line instead: north on Camino Loma Alta, the Hope Camp Trail through the Rincon Mountain District (one of the park's bike-legal trails) to Hope Camp, then down to Rincon Valley where it rejoins the AZT300 (10.7 km). From Rincon Valley to Gardner Canyon the Madrean Rugged Ramble runs on the Arizona Trail, and its points are used as the route (per your preference) - including its 5.6 km detour through Kentucky Camp (water, toilets, camping) on the camp road, where the AZT300 stays on singletrack (kept as an alternative). At Gardner Canyon the MRR leaves for Sonoita (kept as an alternative) and the route stays on the AZT300 line: Casa Blanca Canyon, El Pilar, SR-82 underpass.
- Sonoita Springs Ranch: the 2.8 km of AZT immediately east of the SR-82 underpass (start of Canelo Hills West, towards Hog Canyon) cross private ranch land on a public trail easement - open sunrise to sunset only, no camping, stay on the trail. The route does not ride it (it turns off to Patagonia at the underpass); it only applies if you take the AZT Canelo Hills West alternative.
- At the SR-82 underpass the AZTR 'Legacy AZT to Patagonia' track is used in reverse: Train Track Trail into Patagonia (resupply), then Harshaw Rd and the AZT connector back to the Canelo Hills West passage.
- Canelo Hills West -> Canelo Pass -> Canelo Hills East -> Parker Canyon Lake on the Madrean Rugged Ramble points (it follows the AZT here), then the MRR to Lochiel (border fence), Patagonia (second visit), Salero Canyon, Madera Canyon area, Santa Rita Experimental Range and Tucson (Time Market, 4th Ave).

## Day plan
- Moving time = distance / speed + climb / climbing rate, by surface: singletrack 9.5-12 km/h (Arizona Trail slowest, Fool's Loop fastest; the Bug Spring Trail 5 km/h), dirt roads 13-15 km/h, pavement 20 km/h; climbing 450 m/h on singletrack, 550 on dirt, 700 on pavement. Breaks come on top - December gives ~10 h of daylight.
- The map's Day plan panel splits the route into 18-30 days (default 20) of equal moving time, moves each night to the nearest campground, lodging or hand-picked stop within 15 % of a day, and shows km, climb, moving time and the singletrack / dirt / paved split for every day (also as labels on the map and bands on the elevation profile).

## December notes
- ~10 h of daylight (sunrise ~07:15, sunset ~17:25 in Tucson). The 7 h/day estimate already assumes you ride most of it.
- Summerhaven is at 2,400 m: expect freezing nights and possible snow/ice; the Control Road is unmaintained in winter. Check Coronado NF (Santa Catalina RD) road status before committing. The Catalina Highway is plowed; Bug Spring Trail's upper end sits at ~1,850 m and can hold snow after a storm - stay on the highway to Prison Camp if so.
- Patagonia / Canelo Hills / Parker Canyon Lake sit at 1,300-1,800 m: frosty nights, short days. Parker Canyon Lake marina has limited winter hours.
- Water: AZT rain collectors (Gila canyons, Freeman Rd, Rincon area), Kelvin ADOT spigot, Kearny fill station, Peppersauce, Kentucky Camp, El Pilar, Proctor picnic area. Carry capacity for 50+ km dry stretches.
- Permits: Arizona State Land recreational permit (Fool's Loop and Queen's Ransom cross State Trust land); Usery Mountain / Lost Dutchman / San Tan parks charge entry fees.
